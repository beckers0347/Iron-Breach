## Scale anchors
Player 180 cm. Standard door 240 cm high. Bunker blast door 500 x 400 cm. Hangar bay opening 1,400 x 1,050 cm. Tower stair step rise 20 cm. All buildings modelled in metres, FBX scale 1 (100 uu per m).

## Player start, boundaries, off-limits
- Start: inside the Barracks bunker (SM_BermBunker, zone 02) interior near the front doors, facing the door.
- Playable area: the walled base. Perimeter wall plus dense forest and blocking volumes; hills outside are decorative.
- Off-limits: forest beyond the wall; tower and hangar roofs (no access).
- Known limits of the tower models: the 2 gate towers and the watch tower are climbable via internal spiral stairs to a glazed cabin. The comms tower mast is decorative.
