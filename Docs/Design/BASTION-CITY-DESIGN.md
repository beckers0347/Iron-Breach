# THE BASTION — Carrowgate Citadel
## Hub City Design v1.0 · Aug 2026 · canon-locked to `Story/02_IRONBREACH_Narrative_Bible.md`

> One sentence: **The Bastion is the fortress Carrowgate grew around its wound** — the Breakwater citadel raised on the harbor scar PALAWAN left in M1, where the player lives between deployments, and under whose memorial plinth the oldest lie in the setting has been waiting since 1972.

---

## 1. What the Bastion IS

Three truths, layered like the game's tone:

1. **Gameplay:** the hub. Destiny's Tower grammar in Breakwater fatigues — vendors, mission table, hangar, social space, one continuous walkable place (title spec's rule: *"Bastion should all read as one continuous physical place"*).
2. **Fiction:** Forward Operating Citadel CARROW-1, the Breakwater's anchor garrison for the Carrow Exclusion Zone. Built 2042–2043 into the harbor batteries and the half-collapsed ferry terminal after M1. It is not a shining headquarters; it is a **working fortification that civilians slowly re-inhabited** — the war's hearth (Tone Pillar 4).
3. **Mystery:** the Bastion stands on Carrowgate's 1972 "memorial foundation" — the plinth laid **over the Lantern archive vault** (Bible §XVI, 1972 row; twist T3). The safest place in the city is the lid on its deepest secret. The player walks past the truth every single day for two years of content, and the doc that proves it is filed under maintenance schedules.

**Naming:** soldiers say "the Bastion." Official paperwork says CARROW-1. Civilians say "the Rock." The Pilgrims of the Low Song, holding vigil at its exclusion-side gate, call it "the Stopper" — because it plugs a hole. Nobody official laughs at that anymore. (Official vs. soldier naming as a storytelling channel — Bible §I.2.)

## 2. The Shape of the Place

The Bastion is a **crescent citadel wrapping the harbor scar** — the flooded crater-bay where PALAWAN surfaced. Its open side faces the sea wall and, beyond it, the Carrow Exclusion Zone (the playable Zone One). Everything the player does radiates from one central space, and every district is visible from at least one other (continuity rule; no teleport doors in the fiction, even where load zones exist in the build).

### [1] THE YARD — the heart
Open muster ground under the crescent's arms, floored with salvaged street cobbles from pre-M1 Carrowgate (the city literally underfoot). Function: social hub, fireteam assembly, **mission table** (the Map screen's diegetic home — a horizontal projection table showing the Exclusion Zone, POI pins live). Sgt. Okafor — **Bricks** — runs the Yard from a stacked-crate office she built herself; she is the hub anchor NPC from Y1 (Bible L37) and the voice that reacts to the player's war. Ambient rule: the Yard is never silent and never panicked — radio discipline, cargo movement, somebody's bad music from a workshop. The hearth.

### [2] THE FOUNDRY — the economy
Fabrication wing built into the old ferry terminal's turbine hall. This canonizes the name already shipped in the build (`DA_Item_KaijuChitin`: *"The Foundry pays in clearance for every kilogram"*). Functions: vendors, gunsmith, kaiju-material intake (the loot sink), armor/frame customization benches (title spec's "armor/mech customisation" lives here). Fiction: the Foundry is co-run by the Breakwater and a **Meridian Group** reclamation contract — the war's uncomfortable economics standing right next to the mess hall. Meridian's site rep has a glass office the player can see into. Sometimes the blinds are down. (§V.2 seeding, zero dialogue needed.)

### [3] THE WALL OF NAMES — the heart's scar
The memorial. Every fallen Breakwater name in the Carrow sector, and the physical instantiation of the **Echo Tags** system (Bible §XII): the wall grows as the community recovers tags server-wide — drift made of names. At its base: the **1972 plinth**, a modest granite block predating everything around it, inscribed only *"FOR THOSE THE SEA RETURNED — 1972."* It is the oldest object in the Bastion. It is the lid of the Lantern archive vault. Set-dressing rule: the plinth must appear in the M11 Breakwater Day scene, in at least one trailer frame, and never be lit like it matters (L27 fingerprint discipline — hide it in plain sight for two years).

### [4] THE STEPS — the hearth
Terraced civilian quarter climbing the crescent's eastern arm — market stalls in gun casemates, laundry lines between searchlight masts, the mess hall everyone actually eats at. This is Tone Pillar 4 made walkable: the war has coffee, tax season, pop music. Static's podcast feed plays from a repair-shop radio (and after his on-air Deepfall in Y1 S1, the feed never updates and never deletes — L33; the radio stays on). Kids do swim drills in the old ferry basin every in-game morning.

### [5] THE DRYDOCK — the cathedral
The frame hangar — **the title screen camera already lives here** (SPEC-title-screen: model only what the camera sees; the Bastion grows outward from that hangar shot). One Caryatid bay: gantries, umbilicals, the two-seat cockpit open like a reliquary. Scale rule from the bible: *the giant is a cathedral; the story is the two people inside it* — the Drydock is deliberately the only vertical-awe space in the Bastion, and the player's path to it always passes through the human-scale Yard first. Boarding for mech deployments happens here diegetically; the seasonal set-dressing system (title spec) rotates battle damage, maintenance state, and paint.

### [6] THE UNDERCROFT — the threshold
Armory, barracks, gym (Bricks' furniture everywhere), and the load-bearing secret: **Maintenance Level C**, a corridor with a sealed blast door tagged with a work order that has been "pending" since before the Bastion existed. Door never opens until M14's act (T3 reveal window). Until then it is one locked door among many — but it hums, barely, at nine-beat intervals, and one in a hundred players will notice (whisper-tier, L13).

## 3. Story Anchors (what happens HERE across the saga)

| Beat | Where in the Bastion | Bible ref |
|---|---|---|
| M1 aftermath — the citadel's founding rubble | The Yard's cobbles ARE that rubble | §VII M1 |
| M5 — Lt. Rhodes dies holding Carrow Bridge | Bridge visible from the sea wall; her name is the first the player watches added to the Wall | §VI, M5 |

| Campaign aftermath — all quiet | Mara alone on the sea wall: "—one shore." | §VII |
| Y1 onward — Bricks runs the garrison | The Yard, permanently | L32/L37 |
| Static's Deepfall | The radio in the Steps stops updating | §Y1 S1 |
| T3 — the vault under the plinth | Wall of Names → Undercroft Level C | §VIII T3 |
| Elber's statue "posed catching, not striking" | Outside the Academy, visible on the landward approach | §VI |

**The Bastion's own arc:** Act 1 it is a fortress with civilians in it; by Year 2 it is a town with guns on it. The set dressing makes the argument no cutscene should: holding works. Then ATACAMA's year strips it back to a fortress again — the hearth is the thing the fiction threatens, never cheaply.
